Welcome

Government Degree College for Women, Bapatla has been serving the educational needs of girl students of Bapatla and the surrounding villages since 1972. The college is committed to providing quality, affordable education in a safe and supportive environment.

Our experienced faculty, well-equipped laboratories and library, and an active programme of sports, cultural and service activities help every student grow in knowledge, confidence and character.
