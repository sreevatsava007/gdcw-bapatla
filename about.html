About the College

Serving women's education in Bapatla since 1972

Our History

Government Degree College for Women, Bapatla was established in 1972 to provide accessible higher education to girl students of Bapatla town and the neighbouring rural areas. Over the decades the college has grown in strength and facilities, and thousands of its alumnae now serve society as teachers, professionals, public servants and entrepreneurs.

Vision

To empower young women through quality education that builds knowledge, values and self-reliance.

Mission

  • Provide affordable, high-quality education to every girl student.
  • Encourage academic excellence along with sports, culture and service.
  • Create a safe, inclusive and supportive campus.
